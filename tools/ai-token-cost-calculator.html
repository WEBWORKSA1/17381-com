How it works

A token is a chunk of text — roughly three-quarters of an English word on average, though numbers and code often split into more tokens. Providers publish a price per million input tokens and per million output tokens. Multiply your typical prompt and reply sizes by those prices and by your volume.

The formula

Cost per request = (input tokens × input price + output tokens × output price) ÷ 1,000,000

Worked example

With 1,500 input tokens, 400 output tokens and example prices of $3 / $15 per million, one request costs $0.0105. At 2,000 requests a day that is about $21 a day, $630 a month and $7,665 a year.

How to cut AI costs

  • Trim context: long system prompts and retrieved documents are paid on every call.
  • Use prompt caching where your provider offers it for repeated context.
  • Route by difficulty: send easy tasks to smaller, cheaper models.
  • Cap output length and ask for structured, concise answers.
  • Batch non-urgent jobs if your provider discounts batch processing.
Always verify current pricing on your AI provider's official pricing page — rates change frequently and differ by model.

Frequently asked questions

How many tokens is a word?

For English prose, roughly 1.3 tokens per word on average; numbers, code and non-English text usually use more.

Why is output more expensive than input?

Generating each output token requires a full model step, so providers usually price output higher than input.
