How it works

Count how often the task happens, measure how many minutes the AI genuinely saves each time (after checking and fixing its output), and value that time at the fully loaded hourly cost of the person doing it — salary plus benefits, taxes and overhead.

The formulas

  • Hours saved = Tasks × Minutes saved ÷ 60
  • Value = Hours saved × Loaded hourly cost
  • Net monthly benefit = Value − Tool cost
  • Payback (months) = Setup cost ÷ Net monthly benefit

Worked example

400 support replies a month, 12 minutes saved each, at $45/hour, is 80 hours or $3,600 of time. Minus a $300 tool, the net benefit is $3,300 a month and a $2,500 setup pays back in under a month.

Keep it honest

  • Measure saved minutes after review time, not the raw generation speed.
  • Saved time only becomes money if it is redeployed — to more sales, faster delivery or avoided hiring.
  • Include quality risk: what does an AI error cost, and how often will it happen?

Frequently asked questions

What is a loaded hourly cost?

Gross pay plus employer taxes, benefits and a share of overhead, divided by productive hours. It is often 1.25–1.4× the base hourly wage.

Does saved time always equal saved money?

No. It becomes money when you use the hours for revenue-producing work or avoid extra hiring.
