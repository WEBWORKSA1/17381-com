How it works

The number is split into groups. The international system groups digits in threes (thousands, millions, billions). The Indian numbering system groups the first three digits, then twos: thousand, lakh (100,000) and crore (10,000,000). So 1,73,81,000 in Indian notation is 17,381,000 internationally.

Writing a cheque correctly

  1. Start the words at the far left of the line so nothing can be inserted.
  2. Write cents/paise as words ("and fifty cents") or as a fraction ("and 50/100").
  3. End with "only" and draw a line through any remaining space.
  4. Make sure the figures box and the words match exactly — banks generally rely on the words if they differ.

Examples

  • 17,381 → Seventeen thousand three hundred and eighty-one
  • 1,250,000 → One million two hundred and fifty thousand = Twelve lakh fifty thousand
  • 25,000,000 → Twenty-five million = Two crore fifty lakh

Frequently asked questions

Should I use 'and' in written numbers?

British, Indian and Commonwealth usage includes 'and' (three hundred and eighty-one); US cheque style often omits it. Both are widely accepted.

What is a lakh and a crore?

A lakh is 100,000 and a crore is 10,000,000 in the Indian numbering system.
