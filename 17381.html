In mathematics

17381 is composite. It factors as 7 × 13 × 191 — three different primes — which makes it a sphenic number. It has eight divisors (1, 7, 13, 91, 191, 1337, 2483 and 17381) whose sum is 21,504. Because its proper divisors add up to only 4,123, it is a deficient number.

  • Binary 100001111100101 · Octal 41745 · Hexadecimal 43E5
  • Nearest primes: 17377 below and 17383 above.
  • Written backwards it becomes 18371, which is prime.
  • Digit sum 20, digital root 2; it takes 53 steps to reach 1 under the Collatz rule.
  • Its square root is about 131.84, so checking whether it is prime needs trial division only up to 131.

The hacker easter egg

One of its divisors is 1337 — "leet", the old internet slang for elite. In other words, 17381 = 13 × 1337. A fitting fact for a lab about numbers and code.
