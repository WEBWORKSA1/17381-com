FAQ

Are the calculators really free?

Yes. Every calculator runs in your browser with no sign-up. The site is funded by advertising, sponsors and voluntary supporters.

Is my data stored?

No. Calculator inputs never leave your device. Only forms you choose to submit (like the Check-up) are sent to us.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools give estimates for education and planning. Speak to a qualified professional before making major financial decisions.

Why is the site called 17381?

17381 is our domain name. It is a sphenic number (7 × 13 × 191) and we use it as a neutral, global brand for a lab about numbers. Read the story of 17381.
