Editorial Policy

Last updated 2026-11-15.

Our standards

  • Show the work: every calculator displays its formula and a worked example so results can be checked.
  • Test before publishing: each tool is checked against hand-calculated examples.
  • Plain English: we explain concepts so a busy owner can act on them.
  • Sources: where we reference external facts, we prefer primary or authoritative sources.
  • Review: pages show a last-reviewed date and are updated when methods or facts change.

Independence

Advertisers and sponsors never influence calculator logic or editorial conclusions. Sponsored placements are clearly labelled.

AI assistance

We may use AI tools to help draft or check content. A human editor reviews, tests and approves everything before publication.

Corrections

Spotted a problem? Report it. We correct verified errors promptly.
