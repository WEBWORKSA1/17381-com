# 17381.com — Research & Idea Selection

## 1. What "17381" means (findings)

| Lens | Finding | Commercial weight |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 17381 = 7 × 13 × 191 — a **sphenic number** (product of three distinct primes). 8 divisors: 1, 7, 13, 91, 191, 1337, 2483, 17381. Sum of divisors 21,504 → deficient. Hex `43E5`, binary `100001111100101`, octal `41745`. Neighbouring primes 17377 and 17383. Reversed it is 18371, which is prime. Digit sum 20, digital root 2. Collatz steps: 53. | High — "is X prime / factors of X / X in binary" is a proven programmatic-SEO traffic engine (numbermath.com, numberempire.com, numbersaplenty.com). |
| Fun / culture | 17381 = 13 × **1337** ("leet" — hacker culture). | Shareable hook for branding. |
| Dates | As DD/MM/YY → 17 March 1981. As Unix day 17381 → 3 Aug 2017. As Excel serial 17381 → 2 Aug 1947. | Date-from-number tools are a niche search pattern. |
| Commerce / records | Appears only as generic identifiers (docket numbers, e.g. Louisiana Supreme Court docket No. 17,381 in 1909; blockchain block heights; strain IDs). No brand, product line, ZIP code (17381 is not an assigned US ZIP) or standard owns it. | **No trademark conflict**, clean blank-slate brand. |
| Chinese numerology | 1-7-3-8-1: contains 8 (bā ≈ fā, "prosper"), no 4. Neutral-to-positive for East-Asian buyers. | Resale/brand value for 5N .com. |
| AI | LLM tokenizers split numbers like "17381" into multi-digit chunks — numeracy is a known weak spot for AI, which is why number-checking tools still earn traffic. | Angle for "AI + numbers" content and AI cost/ROI calculators. |
| Domain economics | Only 100,000 five-digit numeric .com strings exist (00000–99999). Short, global, language-neutral. | Scarcity → brandable "number lab" identity. |

**Conclusion:** 17381 has no fixed external meaning — so the domain should *own the idea of numbers themselves*, aimed at the highest-paying audience that searches for numbers: business owners and finance/AI decision makers.

## 4. AdSense readiness checklist (applied)
- 30+ pages of original content; every tool page has 400–700 words of explanation.
- About, Contact, Privacy (with Google ads/cookies wording), Terms, Cookies, Disclaimer, Editorial policy.
- Clear navigation, no broken links, mobile responsive, fast static pages.
- `ads.txt` with `google.com, pub-6620975821265271, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0`.
- No prohibited content, no incentivised ad clicks, ads not placed to mimic navigation; contest is skill-based and free to enter (no lottery).
