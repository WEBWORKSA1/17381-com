How it works

  1. Pick a number. Any number — a price, a statistic, a ratio, a date or 17381 itself.
  2. Find the insight. Use our calculators or your own analysis to show what that number really means for a business, a household budget or an AI project.
  3. Explain it in 300 words or fewer. Add a worked example and, optionally, a link to a chart or a short video.
  4. Submit before the last day of the month. Winners are announced in the first week of the next month.
