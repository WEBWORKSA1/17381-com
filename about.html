About us

About the 17381 Numbers Lab

We build free, honest tools for the numbers that run businesses, money and AI — and explain them in plain English.

Our mission

Most important decisions in a small business come down to a handful of numbers: margin, break-even, runway, the cost of a loan, the payback on a new tool. Yet the people making those decisions are often too busy to build spreadsheets. We make those numbers fast, free and understandable.

What we build

  • Calculators that show their formulas and worked examples — no black boxes.
  • The Number Analyzer, which decodes any whole number in a click.
  • Guides and videos that explain the "why" behind the math.
  • The free Numbers Check-up, a human review of your figures.

How we stay independent

The site is funded by advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, partner introductions that users explicitly request, and voluntary support. Calculator results and editorial content are never influenced by advertisers. Read our editorial policy.

Why the name?

17381 is our domain — a sphenic number, 7 × 13 × 191, with no external meaning to protect. It reads the same in every language. Read the full story.

Accuracy & corrections

Every calculator is tested against hand-worked examples. If you spot an error, please tell us — we fix verified errors quickly and note material changes.
