Official Rules — Monthly Number Challenge

Please read these rules before entering.

1. Sponsor

The Monthly Number Challenge ("Challenge") is run by 17381.com ("we", "us"). Contact us through the contact form.

2. No purchase necessary

No purchase, payment or donation is required to enter or win. Supporting the site does not improve your chances.

3. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are at least 18 years old or the age of majority in their place of residence, where not prohibited by law. Void where prohibited or restricted. Our team members and their immediate families may not win prizes.

4. Entry period

Each Challenge runs for one calendar month, ending at 23:59 UTC on the last day of the month. Entries received later roll into the following month.

5. How to enter

Submit one original entry per person per month through the entry form on the Contests page. Entries must be your own work, in English, 300 words or fewer, and must not infringe anyone's rights or contain unlawful, hateful or misleading content.

6. Judging

This is a game of skill. Entries are judged by our editorial team on usefulness (40%), accuracy (30%), clarity (20%) and originality (10%). Decisions are final. If fewer than three entries meet a reasonable quality standard, we may award fewer prizes.

7. Prizes

Prize amounts are shown on the Contests page for the relevant month and paid electronically within 30 days of confirmation. Prizes are non-transferable. Winners are responsible for any taxes. We may require identity verification and a signed eligibility declaration.

8. Licence to publish

You keep copyright in your entry. By entering, you grant us a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to publish, edit for length and promote your entry with credit to you.

9. Privacy

We use entry information only to run the Challenge and contact you, as described in our Privacy Policy. Winners' names and countries may be published.

10. General

We may cancel, modify or suspend the Challenge if fraud, technical failures or other factors beyond our control affect its integrity. These rules are governed by the laws applicable to the site operator.
