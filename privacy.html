Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-15.

This policy explains what information 17381.com ("we") collects, how we use it and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Calculator inputs: processed in your browser and not sent to us.
  • Forms you submit (Check-up, contact, newsletter, pledges, contest entries, job applications): the details you provide, such as name, email and message. Forms are delivered to our private inbox through the FormSubmit service.
  • Usage data: standard log and cookie data collected by our advertising and analytics partners, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message where required.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube may set cookies under its own policy.

How we use information

  • To answer your requests and deliver the Check-up, newsletter or results you asked for.
  • To run contests, process pledges and review job applications.
  • To introduce you to partners only if you explicitly request it.
  • To improve the site and keep it secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them for, and up to 24 months for business records, unless law requires otherwise.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or PIPEDA), you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Use our contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
