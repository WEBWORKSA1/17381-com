Cookie Policy

Last updated 2026-11-15.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

Cookies we and our partners use

  • Essential / preferences: we store your theme choice and cookie-notice choice in your browser's local storage.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted.
  • Embedded video: YouTube may set cookies after you press play.
  • Form delivery: FormSubmit processes form submissions you choose to send.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, manage ad personalisation at Google Ads Settings, and see aboutads.info for more choices. Blocking cookies does not stop the calculators from working.
